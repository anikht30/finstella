Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, transfer, and disclosure of personal data collected by Finstella ("Company", "Platform", "we", "us", or "our") from users, members, speakers, attendees, and visitors ("User", "you", or "your"). We operate as a Data Fiduciary under Indian privacy regulations.

1. Scope & Regulatory Compliance

We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with Indian legislation, specifically the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. Where applicable, we also maintain standards aligned with international frameworks including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and CCPA/CPRA for foreign members.

2. Information We Collect (Personal Data)

Given the specialized nature of our platform, we collect data directly from you or automatically through your usage:

  • Professional Profile & Verification Data: Full name, corporate entity, professional title, work email address, professional credentials/designations (e.g., ICAI CA membership number, CFA, CPA, ICSI), license numbers, LinkedIn profile links, and professional background.
  • Account Credentials: Usernames, encrypted passwords, communication preferences, and security logs.
  • User Content & Technical Contributions: Forum posts, professional queries, peer answers, tax/accounting updates, uploaded attachments, upvotes, and peer-to-peer messages.
  • Event & Webinar Data: Registration records, attendance logs, Q&A submissions, networking preferences, dietary requirements (for offline physical events), and survey feedback.
  • Financial & Billing Information: Payment transaction history, GSTIN (for Indian tax invoicing), billing address, and transaction logs processed through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe). We do not store raw banking or card credentials on our servers.
  • Technical & System Data: Device IP addresses, device identifiers, browser types, log files, time zone settings, and website interaction metrics.

3. Legal Grounds & Consent Requirements

In accordance with the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your personal data primarily based on your explicit, informed, and unambiguous Consent, or for specified Legitimate Uses:

  • Consent-Based Processing: Managing your account profile, facilitating peer interactions, sending newsletter digests, and organizing event registrations. You may withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Uses / Legal Obligations: Verifying professional qualifications to prevent unauthorized impersonation, fulfilling statutory obligations under the Companies Act, 2013 and Income Tax Act, 1961, ensuring platform security, and preventing financial fraud.

4. How We Use Your Personal Data

Your personal data is used to:

  • Operate the peer knowledge network and display verified professional profiles to other verified members.
  • Verify ICAI / professional credentials to maintain community trust.
  • Issue tax-compliant GST invoices for paid events, subscriptions, or ticketings.
  • Detect, investigate, and mitigate cyber security threats, spam, or unlawful conduct on the platform.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared strictly under these circumstances:

  • Peer Visibility: Profile details and published content are visible to other verified members based on your account privacy settings.
  • Event Partners & Venue Managers: For co-hosted events, attendee rosters (name, designation, corporate entity) may be shared with venue managers for physical security and logistical purposes.
  • Authorized Service Providers: Encrypted data processing by cloud hosting providers (AWS, Microsoft Azure), Indian SMS/email gateways, and payment service providers operating under contractually binding security commitments.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When mandated by law, court orders, or official directives issued by government agencies authorized under Indian law.

6. Data Storage, Localization, and Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest, access controls, and periodic audits. Your personal data is primarily stored and processed on secure cloud servers located within India or compliant international cloud regions adhering to security safeguards.

7. Your Data Rights (Data Principal Rights)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, as a Data Principal, you possess statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data being processed and identities of third parties with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: Request the correction of inaccurate data or the erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit grievances regarding data processing directly to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email our Grievance Officer at grievance@finstella.pro.
