Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, use, storage, and disclosure of personal and professional information collected by Finstella ("Company", "Platform", "we", "us", or "our") from users, members, speakers, attendees, and visitors ("User", "you", or "your") accessing our website, mobile applications, and services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Scope & Regulatory Compliance

We are committed to maintaining privacy standards aligned with global data protection frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable international privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

Given the specialized nature of our platform, we collect information across several categories:

  • Professional Profile & Verification Data: Full name, professional title, corporate entity, work email address, professional credentials/designations (e.g., CPA, CFA, ACCA, CA, CFP), license numbers, LinkedIn profile links, and employment history.
  • Account & Registration Details: Passwords, account preferences, communication settings, and billing addresses.
  • User Content & Peer Interactions: Professional queries posted, responses submitted, technical updates shared, uploaded document attachments, comments, upvotes, and peer-to-peer messages.
  • Event & Webinar Participation Data: Registration records for digital webinars and physical offline events, event attendance logs, questions submitted during Q&A sessions, networking preferences, dietary requirements (for offline events), and post-event survey responses.
  • Payment & Financial Data: Credit card/bank information, transaction histories, tax IDs, and billing metrics processed via PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We do not store raw payment card data on our servers.
  • Automated Device & Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages visited, session durations, and log files.

3. Legal Bases for Data Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver network access, facilitate query exchanges, process event ticketing, and manage user accounts under our Terms & Conditions.
  • Legitimate Interests: To verify professional credentials, ensure forum integrity, prevent spam/fraud, optimize user experience, and send relevant platform updates.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with statutory tax, financial, and regulatory reporting obligations.
  • Consent: For marketing communications, non-essential cookies, or handling sensitive voluntary data.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed to:

  • Facilitate member-to-member knowledge sharing, query posting, and technical discussion.
  • Verify professional qualifications to maintain a high trust-to-noise ratio within the community.
  • Organize, host, and manage virtual webinars and physical networking/educational events.
  • Provide custom technical updates, industry news digests, and tailored query recommendations.
  • Detect, investigate, and mitigate improper platform use, regulatory breaches, or intellectual property infringements.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers. We share data strictly under the following conditions:

  • Peer Visibility: Profile information, posted queries, technical updates, and public forum responses are visible to other verified members according to your privacy settings.
  • Event Partners & Organizers: For co-hosted webinars or offline events, attendee rosters (name, designation, company name) may be shared with event co-hosts, sponsors, or venue providers for security and operational purposes.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS, Azure), email routing services, event streaming platforms (e.g., Zoom), CRM providers, and payment processors bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Mandates: Where required by valid court orders, regulatory subpoenas, or applicable laws.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures, including end-to-end TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access controls, and periodic penetration testing. Data is retained for as long as your account remains active or as required by financial, tax, and statutory record-keeping regulations.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in servers located outside your jurisdiction. When transferring data internationally (e.g., from the EEA/UK to third countries), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and robust organizational safety measures to ensure equivalent protection.

8. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you hold statutory rights including the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing, portability, and object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@finstella.co.
